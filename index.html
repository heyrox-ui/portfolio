The bigger picture

From first click to next step.

Every lead deserves a clear path. Here's an example of how an integrated real estate marketing system can work.

01 · ATTRACT

Campaign or content

A prospect discovers a property or your services through a post or ad.

02 · CAPTURE

Inquiry submitted

A form collects their question and key details with appropriate notice.

03 · ORGANIZE

CRM updated

Contact and opportunity information enters the correct pipeline.

04 · FOLLOW UP

Agent takes action

The assigned agent is notified and can continue the conversation.
